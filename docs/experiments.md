# Experiment definitions

The primary epidemic comparison contains five data-generating settings and five
candidate families. The controlled phase study varies observation times, noise,
and independent initial conditions under UDE truth. Biochemical, ecological,
and electrophysiological studies cover
substrate conversion, predator-prey dynamics, and FitzHugh-Nagumo excitation.
Prediction and confidence use independent evaluation records. See
`configs/submission.toml` for the released repetition counts.
